Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Whether you are stepping onto a yoga mat for the very first time, looking to unwind after a demanding workday, or seeking gentle joint-friendly movement, Riverbend Yoga Studio offers a calm, welcoming space tailored to your pace.

Our Studio Mission

At Riverbend Yoga Studio, our mission is to make evidence-informed movement, mindfulness, and breathwork accessible to beginners, busy professionals, and older adults. We focus on sustainable mobility, stress reduction, and body awareness rather than acrobatic poses.

New to Yoga? Start Comfortably

You do not need to be flexible, athletic, or experienced to join a class. Every session includes clear verbal cues, seated or chair-supported options, and complimentary props such as blocks, straps, and bolsters so you can move safely and confidently.

Featured Class: Gentle & Chair-Supported Yoga

Designed specifically for newcomers and active older adults, Gentle & Chair-Supported Yoga pairs slow, supported stretching with restorative breathing techniques to ease tension in the neck, shoulders, and lower back.
