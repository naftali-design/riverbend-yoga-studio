Accessibility Accommodations

We are committed to ensuring every student can participate safely and comfortably:

  • Step-Free Access: Ground-level entrance, wide doorways, and an accessible restroom with grab bars.
  • Adaptive Props & Seating: Sturdy armless chairs, extra-thick mats, bolsters, blocks, and straps available at no charge.
  • Sensory-Friendly Environment: Fragrance-free studio policy, warm natural lighting, and optional hands-off verbal cueing.
  • Clear Communication: Instructors face the room during demonstrations and provide plain-language options for every posture.

Studio Policies

  • Arrival Time: Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first session so an instructor can help set up your props.
  • Cancellation Window: Group class reservations may be canceled up to 4 hours before start time without penalty.
  • Scent-Free & Quiet Space: Please silence mobile devices before entering the practice room and avoid strong perfumes or scented lotions.
