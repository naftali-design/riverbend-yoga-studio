Classes, Private Sessions & Pricing

Our class offerings are structured to support gradual progress, stress recovery, and functional mobility at every stage of life.

Group Class Types

Gentle & Chair-Supported Yoga

A slow-paced, low-impact class emphasizing joint mobility, balance, and breath coordination. Ideal for beginners, older adults, and anyone recovering from a sedentary week.

Mindful Vinyasa Flow

A steady, breath-linked sequence that builds functional strength, posture, and focus. Instructors offer clear step-by-step progressions so busy professionals can energize without feeling rushed.

Restorative Yoga & Breathwork

Floor-based poses held comfortably for several minutes using bolsters, blankets, and blocks to calm the nervous system and encourage deep physical relaxation.

Every class on our weekly schedule is one of these three class types. Some sessions, such as our midday and weekend flows, are tailored to a specific focus or pace.

Private One-on-One Sessions

Prefer individualized guidance before joining a group class? Our 60-minute private sessions allow you to work directly with a certified instructor to build a personalized sequence around your comfort level, mobility goals, or schedule.
